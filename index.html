Our Story

A True Neighborhood Italian Joint

Serving Dallas Since 2010

Do you remember a time when restaurants didn't have "BS" split charges, 18% gratuity added, or ridiculous statements like "I'm sorry, that's our policy?" Welcome to Kenny's Italian Kitchen — a restaurant reminiscent of the way things used to be.

Modeled after the Little Italy neighborhoods of Boston, Philadelphia, and New York, Kenny's is decorated with red-checkered tablecloths, dark woods, and walls adorned with black-and-white photos of friends, family, and regular guests. You'll swear you just walked into a neighborhood joint in the Northeast.

"We make it our policy to have no policy. Every guest leaves happy — that's the only rule."

Chef Kenny Bowers, partners Bob Stegall and Clay McAfee, and Managing Partner Liz Mardeda have created a place where the food, the people, and the atmosphere make every visit feel like home.
